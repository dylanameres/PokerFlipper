PokerFlipper: a free Texas Hold'em & Omaha table simulator

PokerFlipper deals poker hands right in your browser. Pick Texas Hold'em or Omaha, deal the hole cards, then flip the flop, turn and river to see who wins. Finished hands are saved to the hand history.

Play poker online with friends

Create a private room and share the room code. The deck is shuffled on the server, so neither browser ever sees undealt cards, and each player's hole cards stay hidden until the river. No account or download needed.

Daily poker puzzle

PokerFlipper Daily is a new poker puzzle every day: guess the hidden five-card hand in six tries, Wordle-style. Everyone gets the same hand. Solve it, keep your streak going, and share your result.
